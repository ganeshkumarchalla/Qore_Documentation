Forge

Forge is QORE’s proof-aware optimization workspace. It proposes or evaluates circuit transformations and accepts a candidate only when its unitary behavior and terminal measurement mapping remain equivalent within tolerance.

Why proof matters

A circuit with fewer gates is not automatically correct. Removing or changing an operation can alter phase, entanglement, or classical output. Forge compares behavior rather than trusting appearance.

What to review

Compare gate counts, depth, unitary equivalence, measurement mapping, source representation, and warnings. Preserve both original and candidate evidence in the Ledger when a transformation matters.
