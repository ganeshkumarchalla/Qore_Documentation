EVIDENCE

Quantum Ledger

The Ledger preserves experiments as evidence, not just screenshots. Each record retains source identity, backend, shots, seed, noise model, execution time, timestamp, TVD, warnings, metrics, versions, and output.

Reproducibility

Re-run an experiment using the same source and configuration. A seed makes Aer sampling deterministic; version and source fingerprints show whether the environment changed.

Review

Use historical snapshots to compare experiments, inspect a transformation’s provenance, and export JSON for external analysis. Do not treat a result as reliable without its configuration and source context.
