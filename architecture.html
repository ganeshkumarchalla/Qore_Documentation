How QORE works

QORE follows an evidence-driven flow: author, constrained parser, QORE IR, isolated worker, Qiskit Aer, evidence model, Lab, Forge, and Ledger.

Backend

FastAPI provides request contracts and guards. The quantum engine parses safe circuit source, builds QORE IR, simulates with Aer, calculates metrics, emits QASM, and validates transformations. Bounded workers isolate execution. SQLite stores projects and experiments.

Frontend

The React workspace brings together Explorer, editor, Quantum Lab, Forge, Ledger, Terminal, State World, and Bloch visualizations.
