SIMULATION

Simulation and evidence

QORE runs local statevector and shot-based simulations through Qiskit Aer. Choose shots and a seed, then compare measured counts with ideal probability.

Lab views

Distribution compares sampled and ideal results. State World exposes basis outcomes. Bloch views render single-qubit states. Amplitudes, unitary matrices, and OpenQASM provide mathematical evidence.

Forge and Ledger

Forge accepts circuit changes only after unitary and measurement equivalence checks. Ledger archives source hash, configuration, output, warnings, versions, and run history.
