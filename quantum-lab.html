Quantum Lab

Quantum Lab is the controlled execution surface for a QORE circuit. It accepts the parsed QORE IR, executes it through the bounded local runtime, and organizes evidence into distribution, State World, Bloch, unitary, amplitude, and QASM views.

Configure a run

Shots determine how many sampled measurements are collected. A seed makes sampled output repeatable. Optimization level controls transpilation. Noise settings model non-ideal behavior. Record these settings because they affect the output and its interpretation.

Read results

The distribution view compares measured counts to ideal statevector probability. Execution metadata records backend, timing, seed, noise model, and timestamp. TVD indicates how much finite sampling diverges from ideal reference.

Use it well

Start with small circuits. Validate a single gate, then a controlled gate, then measurement mapping. Increase shots only after the circuit logic is correct. Use warnings and terminal output as evidence, not as decoration.
